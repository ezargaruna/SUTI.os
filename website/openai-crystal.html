a confusing letter · непонятное письмо

you want to know whether you need to do anything today.

OpenAI tools · try this

ask ChatGPT: “explain this made-up notice in two sentences. what is the next step, and what is still unclear?”

SUTI · try this

in Verenium, choose order. put the known date in one box and your next step in another.

⟡ check the difference

“the library closes on Friday for repairs.” do you know when it reopens?

no. the notice says when it closes, not when it reopens. ask the library rather than inventing a date.

объясни объявление двумя фразами. библиотека закрывается в пятницу; дата открытия неизвестна.

words for a child · слова для ребёнка

you and a child want to ask for a quiet minute in a noisy room.

OpenAI tools · try this

an adult can ask ChatGPT for two short phrases, then read them aloud together. no account or personal story from the child is needed for this exercise.

SUTI · try this

in the language game, try “can we sit somewhere quiet?” or make up another line. the child chooses whether to use it.

⟡ check the difference

if the child says “not now”, is the scene unfinished?

no. stopping is a complete move.

вместе со взрослым придумай фразу «можно потише?». можно и не играть.

an invitation to help · предложить помощь

a neighbour may need groceries; you want to offer without deciding for them.

OpenAI tools · try this

ask ChatGPT to turn “you need my help” into a question that allows refusal.

SUTI · try this

synaura: offer one specific thing. “i’m going to the shop. shall i bring anything?”

⟡ check the difference

nobody replied. can you record a yes?

no. record “no reply”. don’t arrange a delivery on their behalf.

предложи одно дело. нет ответа — нет договорённости.

a small change to a site · проверить исправление

a maker wants a visitor to stop a game without losing their words.

OpenAI tools · try this

ask Codex to add a finish button and show the test that proves returning preserves the draft. review the change before release.

SUTI · try this

VereNITYA: write the criterion first. enter one line, finish, return and compare it with the original.

⟡ check the difference

the button exists. does that prove the words survived?

no. exercise the return path and inspect the actual text.

проверяй действие, а не только наличие кнопки.

one shared decision · принять решение вместе

a group has two meeting times and one person has not answered.

OpenAI tools · try this

ask ChatGPT to summarise only the votes actually given, separating objections, abstentions and no reply.

SUTI · try this

нейровече: note the proposal, named commitments with permission, open questions and review date. people make the decision.

⟡ check the difference

two people support a time. one is silent. is the vote unanimous?

no. two supporters and one missing answer. ask whether another time is possible.

не превращай молчание в согласие.

02 · look closer

what belongs on which side?

Verenium offers a question; synaura leaves room to decline. a person decides. Syntaxium labels what is known; LinguaFUSION tries the words aloud; VereNITYA checks the outcome. the council records different voices without manufacturing a group decision.

a possible SUTI review loop

design proposal, not a description of OpenAI’s internal architecture.

  1. synaura: ask what the person wants to do and whether to continue.
  2. Syntaxium: label observation, interpretation and unknown separately.
  3. LinguaFUSION: test the same intent in two languages, including “no” and “not now”.
  4. Verenium: let a person choose one next step or leave.
  5. нейровече: record disagreement and silence separately, then let a human decide.
  6. VereNITYA: record the result, scope, date and what would change our conclusion.
