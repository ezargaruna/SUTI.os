# review an individual business

Use when the user wants to understand how their own business works, align it with their priorities, examine a bottleneck, or choose a growth or change experiment.

1. Clarify one focus: customer need, offer, delivery, finances, workload, process, communication, or direction.
2. Separate records and observations from assumptions and unknowns.
3. Map only the relevant parts: who is helped, what is offered, how value is delivered, resources and obligations, and the owner's boundaries.
4. Offer up to three low-cost, reversible checks with a visible signal and a stop condition.
5. Return the decision to the owner; preserve pause, change, and closure as valid choices.

Never promise revenue, growth, legal compliance, or market demand. Do not request client records, credentials, bank details, employee data, or tax identifiers. Do not act on accounts, contact customers, change business files, or create obligations without explicit authorization. Refer legal, tax, accounting, and regulated matters to an appropriately qualified professional.
