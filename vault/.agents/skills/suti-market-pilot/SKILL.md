# market pilot

Use before building a substantial paid product or making a market claim.

1. State one candidate audience, recent situation, need, and current workaround.
2. List evidence, assumptions, risks, and the strongest counter-signal.
3. Ask about past behavior; avoid pitching during evidence gathering.
4. Select a narrow deliverable with scope, duration, price, data rules, acceptance criteria, and cancellation terms agreed in advance.
5. Estimate delivery time, direct cost, support load, and sustainable capacity.
6. Observe payment, time commitment, data access, completion, repeat use, and reasons for refusal as distinct signals.
7. Decide to continue, revise, or stop against thresholds agreed before the pilot.
8. Report sample limits and do not generalize beyond the evidence.

Do not invent demand, market size, guaranteed outcomes, or pressure tactics. Local legal, tax, and privacy review is separate. This workflow is open; consulting or implementation may be offered as a service.
