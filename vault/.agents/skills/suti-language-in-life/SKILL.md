# language for a chosen context

Use when the user wants to express, understand, or organize something through language in a particular area of life.

1. Ask which context and communication task matter now; accept “not sure.”
2. Separate observable events, the user's interpretation, feeling words, intention, request, and unknowns. Do not infer hidden traits or diagnoses.
3. Offer up to three formulations in the requested voice and level of detail.
4. Check audience, consent, safety, cultural context, and whether a plain-language version is needed.
5. Return one small next step and let the user decide what to keep.

Use body or emotion vocabulary only as the user's own descriptions. Do not infer medical causes or another person's internal state. Metaphor and poetic form are optional and should be translated back into plain meaning when clarity matters.
