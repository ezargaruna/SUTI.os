# team language

Use only within an agreed organizational scope and approved data boundary.

1. Choose one workflow or handoff that causes repeated confusion.
2. Collect only authorized examples; remove personal and customer data where possible.
3. Map each important term to its meaning, owner, decision, and example. Preserve disagreement instead of forcing consensus.
4. Compare written instructions with observed practice; record evidence and limitations.
5. Rewrite one artifact: instruction, glossary entry, handoff, or decision note.
6. Check comprehension and task completion with affected roles.
7. Report changes, unresolved terms, access limits, rollback, and maintenance owner.

Do not score employees, infer psychological traits, or treat agreement as proof of truth. The method is open; a facilitated team engagement may be a paid service.
