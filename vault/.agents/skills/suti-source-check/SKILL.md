# source check

Use when a task depends on facts, attribution, rights, safety, current information, or a claim that may be published.

1. Break the claim into checkable parts.
2. Prefer the original document, official source, or direct evidence.
3. Record source, date, scope, and what it does not establish.
4. Distinguish `verified`, `partial`, `unknown`, and `not supported`.
5. Keep source, interpretation, recommendation, and decision separate.
6. If rights, privacy, or attribution remain unclear, mark `hold` and exclude the material from public output.

A citation does not establish endorsement, permission to adapt, or legal compliance. Never present an inference as a sourced fact.
