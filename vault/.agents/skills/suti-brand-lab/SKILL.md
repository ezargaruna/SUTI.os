# brand lab

Use for a specific brand or offer question. The method is open; agree scope and data handling before using client material.

1. Intake: desired decision, audience, constraints, source material, confidentiality, rights, and non-goals.
2. Evidence map: separate observed behavior, direct statements, interpretation, hypothesis, and unknown.
3. Audience situations: identify candidate needs from real episodes and current alternatives; do not invent a persona as evidence.
4. Brand crystal: audience situation, useful outcome, position, proof, language, behavior, values, limits, and next action.
5. Optional four-lens review: practical, social, inner, and wider-value experience. Label symbolic or aspirational claims; do not present them as effects.
6. Content system: audience question → one useful distinction → example → invitation; choose a sustainable cadence and a feedback signal.
7. Test: conduct a small, consent-based review or paid pilot; define what would support, weaken, or stop the proposition.
8. Handoff: provide editable files, assumptions, evidence links, decisions, exclusions, and a return path.

Never promise increased revenue without evidence. Do not use private stories, names, or likenesses without explicit permission. This workflow does not authorize account changes, customer contact, or publication. A paid service may provide facilitation, but the algorithm is open.
