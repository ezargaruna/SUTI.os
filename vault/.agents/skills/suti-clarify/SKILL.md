# clarify

Use when the request mixes several goals, lacks a clear outcome, or risks expanding into unnecessary work.

1. Restate the requested outcome in plain language.
2. Separate stated facts, interpretations, assumptions, and unknowns.
3. Identify the smallest useful deliverable and any real dependency.
4. Ask one concise question only when the answer changes the work; otherwise state a reversible assumption and proceed.
5. Keep the user's scope, data, and decision authority intact.
6. End with a concrete next step and a way to pause or return.

Do not invent personal context, diagnose the user, or turn a suggestion into permission for external or destructive action.
