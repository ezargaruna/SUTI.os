A proposed open programme supporting useful initiatives: learning, accessible tools, mutual aid and care for the living environment. The name is provisional; participation and angel imagery are optional.

The local preparation cycle began on 13 November 2026. Day 90 is 10 February 2027. Registration, jurisdiction, trustees, banking, donation rules and tax treatment remain UNKNOWN; financial operations have not been activated.

Open request without sensitive details → check alignment with purpose and available resources → decision without conflicts of interest → bounded support → evidence of outcome without exposing recipients → appeal and correction.

Programme accountability; finance and independent reconciliation; data protection and participant safety; language editors; pilot coordinators; independent handling of appeals. Roles are proposed; no people have been appointed.

Track consented participation, completed useful actions, response time, accessibility, corrections and actual expenditure. Targets and numerical thresholds are agreed before the pilot; virality and worldwide reach are not guaranteed.

A discrepancy between money and reporting, a consent breach, a leak, a complaint about harm or lack of an accountable person pauses the relevant process for review and a human decision.
