This plan creates a shared working frame for a proposed life support fund and agency of care angels. The agency would coordinate voluntary educational and community projects. The fund would support selected projects through transparent, accountable decisions after a lawful receiving and disbursing arrangement is established. “Angels” is optional imagery for care; people can participate in ordinary language. Support must never depend on adopting SUTI language, a spiritual identity, publicity, content creation, recruitment, a token or a paid product.

The 90-day goal is a tested, documented operating model and an evidence-based decision about a small pilot. A registered foundation, available funds, participant consent, partner acceptance, local teams and a worldwide operating network have not been established by this document. Global reach is a direction of development; every local launch has its own accountable people, language review and context review.

the useful first offer

Start with three optional, modest pathways: (1) open learning materials and practical language support; (2) small collaborative creative projects and accessible tools; (3) support for local initiatives whose purpose, costs and accountable lead can be verified. Scope is selected with the people concerned. Do not promise emergency response, clinical care, legal representation, guaranteed grants or guaranteed income. Where a need exceeds the agency's competence, a human coordinator explains the limit and, with permission, offers a locally verified referral.

Success is defined by what participants can understand and use: a clearer next step, an accessible resource, a completed chosen activity, or a transparently documented project result. Followers, virality and symbolic ranks are secondary communication measures. They do not determine who deserves support.

fund and resource model

The first budget is a decision template. Currency, available balance, donor commitments, bank/host, costs, tax treatment and spending authority are UNKNOWN. Do not invent a fundraising target or claim that SUTI.coin can fund an obligation. Symbolic coins and ranks remain separate from money, grant eligibility and purchasing rights.

Prepare three scenarios: no external money (author time, existing open tools, optional in-kind contribution); a capped pilot (verified committed resources, modest delivery costs, contingency); continuation (cost per useful completed activity, supervision, editing, operations and reserve). Populate real quotes, units and accountable owners before choosing a scenario. budget-template.csv has deliberately blank amounts; a blank is not zero.

For each actual transaction, a lawful operator would retain a restricted evidence record with purpose, permitted funding source, amount/currency, approval, payee verification appropriate to risk, supporting document, reconciliation and retention date. The public view would show aggregate categories and outcomes, without beneficiary names or private circumstances. A proposed two-person approval rule, conflict recusal, restricted-fund tracking and monthly reconciliation require owner adoption and technical implementation. No spending authority is created here.

Proposed grant workflow: explain the offer in plain language → accept only necessary information through an approved route → check scope and conflicts → human review against published criteria → record a reasoned decision and a way to question it → agree a proportionate reporting method → authorize/disburse only through an approved financial route → verify the chosen result → close or revise. Rejection must not imply a judgment of a person's worth. Learning resources can remain open regardless of grant selection.

Proposed criteria: relevance to the agreed purpose, participant choice, practical benefit, feasible delivery, transparent costs, responsible care, and ability to evidence results without exposing people. Urgency and access barriers may be considered by competent humans in the chosen pilot context; automated scoring must not determine assistance.

public collaboration and private evidence

.vault contains the shareable method, empty templates, synthetic scenarios, reviewed learning material and aggregate reports. Personal applications, contact details, banking evidence, complaints, incidents, participant stories and private correspondence belong in a separately governed restricted workspace. A hidden folder inside a public vault does not provide that separation. No operational participant database is created by this plan.

Participation, volunteer work, interviews, recordings, AI processing, testimonials and publication are separate choices. A refusal or withdrawal never reduces unrelated support. Every live intake would first identify its controller, purpose, minimum fields, access, retention/deletion, reply route and applicable legal basis; those are currently UNKNOWN. Start with a blank synthetic case, verify export/correction/deletion, then decide whether real intake is justified. Do not upload the private vault or use application narratives for model training.

Age labels such as 0+, 12+, 18+, 21+, 33+ and 55+ are proposed audience views, not official age ratings or permission for minors to participate. A youth pathway requires competent human oversight, locally checked consent/guardian requirements, safeguarding, moderation and age-appropriate content. Until that arrangement exists, rehearse with adults and synthetic material only; do not open youth intake. Adult participation also retains pause, skip, correction and exit.

decisions required before real operation

gates.v1.json is the explicit evidence ledger. All operational gates start UNKNOWN/HOLD: jurisdiction and lawful operator; accepted governance; financial route; fundraising/transfer assessment; privacy controller and tested data lifecycle; safeguarding/complaints/referrals; delivery capacity; reviewed language and accessibility; publication allowlist; and a bounded pilot authorization. These are project gates proposed here, not a statement that every country has identical legal requirements.

A gate passes only with a named human decision, dated evidence and a defined scope. A registration certificate would not by itself prove fundraising permission everywhere, bank access, tax relief, care competence or the right to process participant data. Verify applicable requirements in the selected jurisdiction and relevant operating countries with competent local advice before making those claims.

evidence and learning

Weekly review records: tasks completed with artifacts; unresolved decisions; expenditure evidence where expenditure exists; chosen activities completed; usefulness feedback voluntarily given; access barriers; complaints and response times; incidents; corrections; and local-language review status. A zero means measured zero, not missing information. Public reports use aggregates and suppress small or identifying groups. Feedback is optional; no testimony, recruitment or promotional video is required in exchange for help.

Proposed pilot ceiling, to be approved: one accountable local setting, one chosen non-clinical learning/support offer, five to ten consenting adult participants, two trained coordinators with backup, a capped verified budget or no-money operation, and a defined two-week period. These are design limits, not enrollment or a promised launch. If staffing or support is unavailable, retain the synthetic rehearsal and revise the model.

Day 90 decision :: continue within demonstrated capacity; revise and run another bounded cycle; or pause/close while honoring commitments, completing agreed payments lawfully, returning restricted funds where required, handling records and explaining the outcome. Scaling is earned separately in each setting. No publication, outreach, payment, registration or live intake is executed by creating this plan.

source notes

These primary sources inform the proposed safeguards; no certification, endorsement, legal advice or partnership is claimed.

  • Core Humanitarian Standard, 2024 provides a framework centred on people's rights, participation and accountable support. The proposed feedback, complaints and responsible-resource practices are design applications, not demonstrated CHS compliance.
  • ICRC handbook on data protection in humanitarian action treats protection of personal data as part of protecting life, dignity and integrity. The proposed minimum-data and restricted-evidence architecture draws on that principle; lawful processing still requires context-specific assessment.
  • FATF, Best Practices on Combating the Abuse of Non-Profit Organisations, published 16 November 2023, describes focused, proportionate measures and clarifies that Recommendation 8 applies to the relevant NPO subset, not automatically every nonprofit. This plan therefore asks for a context-specific assessment rather than a universal compliance claim.
