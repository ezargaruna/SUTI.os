Information sovereignty means understanding where data comes from and choosing access, export and deletion of your own records. Linguistic, educational, creative, economic and organisational autonomy develops through understandable skills and verifiable decisions; achievement is not promised.

The public space contains methods, general instructions and reviewed examples. Personal records, contacts, correspondence and sensitive information are kept separately and excluded from shared exports.

Authorship, visibility, licence and permitted actions are defined separately for each contribution. No applicable licence for the entire legacy collection has been established.
