# Start here

Choose one question. You can skip anything, stop at any time, or change your mind.

1. What is happening?
2. What do I know directly?
3. What am I interpreting or still unsure about?
4. What would help me check?
5. What is one possible next step?

Keep the note local. Do not include another person's private information without a clear reason and their permission.

**SUTI route:** notice → distinguish → relate → verify → decide → act → trace → adapt.
