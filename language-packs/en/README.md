# SUTI.os · English entry edition

This is a partial English entry layer for the open SUTI.os vault. The wider library is currently in Russian; this archive does not claim to be a complete translation.

Start with `start.md`. Keep notes on your device and share only the material you choose. SUTI is a revisable working aid, not a diagnosis, authority, or substitute for professional care.

## A clear working route

Notice what is happening → distinguish what is known from what is interpreted → name the question → check what can be verified → choose a small next step → keep only a useful trace.

Facts, observations, interpretations, hypotheses, authorial images, and unknowns remain distinct. A symbol is not evidence. An unknown is an honest boundary.

## Language adaptation

English terms are chosen for clarity and practical use, not to claim that English speakers share one cognitive or civilizational pattern. Cultural readings remain optional hypotheses tied to sources and context.
